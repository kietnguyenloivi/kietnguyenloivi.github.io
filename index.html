Case study

How we won 6+ sponsors for a student-run event

Task
Find sponsors for both the decoration and the drinks of a 140-guest evening, working alongside the event director.
Approach
I started by writing the sponsorship proposal. With it in hand, the event director and I visited local shops across Würzburg in person to explain what the event was, how big it would be, and what their support would make possible. For the beverage brands, the pitch took place online, so I supported the event director by building the slide deck and pulling together results from earlier editions, such as Global Village 28 and 29, to show the event's track record.
Result
6+ sponsors on board. Local shops donated pens, paper, banners and other materials for the decoration, while Red Bull, Jägermeister and a Pilsner brand supplied the drinks, including 100+ Jägermeister shots and cans of Red Bull.
  • Leadership. From the first concept all the way to event day, I led more than five functional teams, including Event, Research, Risk Management, PR and Content.
  • Participants. On the night, we welcomed students from the United States, China, South Korea, Türkiye, Taiwan, France, Italy, Spain, Germany and more, together with professors.
  • Delivery. I set the timeline for each team, managed on-site logistics and guest flow, and stepped in to solve problems as they came up.
Case study

Planning a 140-guest evening with the event director

Task
Turn the concept into a plan that would actually work, while taking responsibility for risk on the day.
Approach
Together with the event director, I coordinated the teams and how they worked with each other. I then built a timed schedule for every act and planned the decoration and layout of the venue. On the day itself, I monitored the event from start to finish.
Result
100% incident-free across 140+ participants, from students of many cultures to professors, with risk on the day under my direct responsibility.
